Undelegate from validators

Takes HYPE off a validator and into your undelegated balance.

Connect a wallet to see your stakes.

Withdraw to spot

Starts the seven-day queue. Your HYPE earns nothing while it waits, and arrives in spot automatically.

Move to HyperEVM

Moves HYPE or USDC from your HyperCore spot balance to the same address on HyperEVM, usually within a few seconds. HYPE arrives as the native gas token; USDC arrives as the USDC token. Keep a little HYPE on HyperEVM to pay gas there.

Transfers may fail while your account is not in manual mode.

Connect a wallet to see your balance.

Send HYPE or USDC

Sends on Hyperliquid itself, arriving in seconds with no gas. The recipient needs a Hyperliquid account to see it, and any EVM address works.

Sends may fail while your account is not in manual mode. Switch to manual above, send, then switch back if you like.

Connect a wallet to see your balance.
Every action is signed in your wallet and sent straight to Hyperliquid. Nothing is stored here. Use the wallet that owns the stake; API wallets can't unstake.
